Audit a workflow free Get the findings fixed
01

Validate required inputs

Reject or normalize missing fields and unexpected types before they reach another system.

02

Define duplicate behavior

Use an idempotency key or document whether a replay should ignore, update, or create.

03

Protect webhooks

Configure authentication or explicitly document why public access is safe.

04

Keep secrets out of JSON

Use credential references and inspect code, headers, pinned data, and notes before sharing.

05

Bound every retry

Retry only temporary failures, with a limit and delay that will not amplify an outage.

06

Set timeout expectations

External calls should fail observably instead of remaining ambiguous indefinitely.

07

Route terminal errors

Do not let critical nodes silently continue and make the execution look successful.

08

Test the failure path

Exercise invalid input, temporary failure, permanent failure, timeout, and alert failure.

09

Verify downstream records

A green execution is not enough; confirm the intended business record was created exactly once.

10

Write the rollback first

Keep a dated export, rollback trigger, responsible operator, and replay plan.
